# GYM ARENA — Product Vision

## One line
A monthly **"Most Improved" championship** inside the gym: coaches log their clients' results daily from their phones, and a live TV leaderboard turns progress into a public, exciting competition.

## Why it exists
- Members train harder and more consistently when progress is visible and competitive.
- The brand principle — *"the exercise that doesn't get heavier = failed progress"* — becomes the competition rule: **you win by improving, not by already being strong.**
- Coaches stay involved in every client's progress.
- The gym gets a live, premium sports-broadcast atmosphere on its TV.

## Core idea
- **One championship per calendar month**, one global leaderboard, **top 3 win** (prize: membership discount / free month — handled outside the system).
- Every member competes **against their own previous best**, so men/women, young/old, light/heavy compete on one board with no normalization formulas.
- **7 categories, 1000 points:** Deadlift 100 · Bench 100 · Squat 100 · Pull-ups 150 · Push-ups 150 · Treadmill 200 · Consistency 200.
- **Full trust in coaches:** coaches log, results count immediately, no approval queue.

## Users
| User | What they need |
|---|---|
| **Coach** (phone, on the gym floor) | Log a result in ~10 seconds while standing next to the client |
| **Admin** (gym owner) | Open the month, fix typos, close the month, run the winner reveal |
| **Members** (watching the TV) | See their name move up, see new bests happen live, see who's winning |

## Success looks like
- ≥ 70% of registered participants have at least one entry per week.
- Coaches log without complaining about speed (target: median entry < 15 s).
- The monthly reveal becomes an event members show up for.
- Zero disputes about how points were calculated (formula is simple and visible).

## Non-goals (v1)
- Member accounts / member app.
- Result verification, anti-cheating, approval workflows.
- Coach rankings or coach competition.
- Multi-branch.
- Payments, prizes, memberships management.
- Integration with the gym's check-in system.
