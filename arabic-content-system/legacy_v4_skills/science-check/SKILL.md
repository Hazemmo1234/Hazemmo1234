# Science check (stage 7)

Follow `system/SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`.

1. **Claim ledger:** list every claim (spoken and on screen) in a table with columns:
   claim | label | source | what the source actually says | verdict | fix.
2. **Verify** with PubMed tools (`search_articles`, then `get_article_metadata`) or the journal
   page. Check n, population, comparison, outcome and follow-up against the wording.
3. **Correct.** Overstatement means resizing the claim, not adding a long disclaimer.
   Examples: "useless" → the actual condition; "halves injuries" → "programmes that include
   it"; relative → absolute numbers.
4. **Non-science facts** (sports history): keep only what the cited sources support, keep the
   «ممنوع يتقال» list, and label them CARRIED if you didn't re-verify them.
5. **Write the «مش بنقول» notes** for anything a viewer could wrongly infer.
6. **Report** what you verified, what you couldn't reach, and any remaining uncertainty.
   Never fabricate a citation, DOI or number. If you can't verify it, say so.
