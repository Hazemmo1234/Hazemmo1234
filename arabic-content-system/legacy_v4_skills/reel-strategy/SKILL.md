# Reel strategy (stages 1–2)

## Step 1: the viewer as a situation
Write who is watching as a lived moment, not a demographic.
- Weak: «الرياضيين». Strong: «لاعب كورة كل كام شهر يحس بشد في الخلفية وهو بيجري سرعة».
- Name the frustration in the viewer's words, and what they currently believe or do.

## Step 2: find the interesting conflict
Look for one of these. Write which one it is.
- Common advice vs evidence (stretching protects you vs the review data)
- Symptom vs mechanism (pain gone vs capacity back)
- Feeling vs measurement (I feel fine vs single-leg control)
- Effort vs specificity (warm-up done vs warm-up that matches the sport)
- A true story that makes the principle concrete

## Step 3: generate 2–3 angles, then choose
For each angle note: the surprise, the evidence it rests on (with its label from
`system/SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`), the practical takeaway, and how generic it is (would 10
other coaches post this exact take?). Choose the angle with the best surprise-to-evidence
ratio. Clarity, a demonstration or a useful distinction can beat controversy.

## Step 4: core idea in one sentence
Format: «[المشاهد] فاكر [X]، بس [Y] — وده معناه [action]». Write it in plain Egyptian, as
something Hazem would actually say. If you can't write it in one sentence, the reel is two reels.

## Evidence honesty
Separate three things and label them in the worknote:
- **Verified** (checked source), **hypothesis** (reasonable but unchecked), **creative**
  (framing). Never present search volume, trends, competitor performance or audience
  research that wasn't actually observed. If you didn't check, write «مش متحقق منه».
