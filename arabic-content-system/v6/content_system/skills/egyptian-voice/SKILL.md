# Egyptian voice

Read `egyptian_arabic_style_guide.md`.
- Draft in one-breath sentences with varied rhythm, concrete before abstract, the viewer as «انت».
- Read every line aloud. Rewrite anything that stumbles, needs a second breath, or is unclear on one hearing.
- Check the caps: contrast ≤1 · colon ≤2 · ellipsis ≤2 · no fragment runs · no MSA connectors · no clichés.
- Don't add slang or jokes to sound Egyptian. Natural means clear and conversational.
- A protected line stays only if it's still the best line in the new structure.
