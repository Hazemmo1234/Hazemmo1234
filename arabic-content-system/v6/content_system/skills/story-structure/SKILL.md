# Story structure

1. Write a **beat list** with seconds (≈1.95 spoken words/s, calibrated in `content_system/tools/calibration.json`). Each beat gets exactly one job: question · stakes · setback · turn · consequence · mechanism · proof · demonstration · action · insight.
2. **Progression test:** cover each beat in turn and ask what the viewer learns *only* there. If the answer is nothing, merge or cut the beat.
3. **Promise map:** list every question the opening and middle raise, and where each is answered.
4. **Order by ear:** a listener hears it once. Context comes before detail, cause before effect, and who before when.
5. Story shapes: `content_system/guides/sports_injury_storytelling.md` §2. Educational shapes: `content_system/guides/structures_educational.md`. In one batch, no shape is used by more than half the scripts.
