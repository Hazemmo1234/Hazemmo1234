# Strategy

1. **Viewer as a situation**, not a demographic: «لاعب كورة الشد الخلفي بيرجعله كل موسم»، «مشجع فاكر نهائي 2008 بس مايعرفش اللي حصل قبله».
2. **Reason to exist, in one sentence:** what will the viewer understand or feel that they didn't before? If you can't write it, the reel isn't ready.
3. **Find the real conflict:** advice vs evidence · symptom vs capacity · feeling vs measurement · (story) want vs obstacle · expectation vs outcome · a true paradox.
4. **2–3 angles.** For each: the surprise, the evidence label, the payoff, and how generic it is. Pick the best surprise-to-evidence ratio. Clarity or a demonstration can beat controversy.
5. **Label honestly:** verified / hypothesis / creative. Never claim audience data, trends or competitor results that weren't observed.
