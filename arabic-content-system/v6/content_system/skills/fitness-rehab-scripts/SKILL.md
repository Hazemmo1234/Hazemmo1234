# Fitness and rehab scripts

## Explainers (35–50 s)
1. **Open on a lived scene** («بتجري على كورة طويلة، وفجأة إيدك على ورا رجلك») or a do-it-now action («اقف قدام حيطة…»), not on a definition.
2. **Mechanism in plain words**, with the concrete moment before the term.
3. **One proof beat** (a study number, absolute when possible, n on screen) that escalates the idea. It doesn't just decorate it.
4. **Correct the myth once** (≤1 contrast).
5. **End on one action or test** the viewer can do or ask. A referral only with a named red flag (≤1 per batch).
6. Caveats go on screen. Keep the honest qualifier in one spoken clause if it's needed.

## «مالوش لازمة» series (≤25 s)
- Keep the refrain hook; the next line must qualify it within 2 seconds.
- Each condition is concrete, true at the stated strength, and **unique across episodes** (regression-checked).
- Give each episode a different internal shape (condition list · condition + proof · myth → nuance · data → twist).
- Close with the signature line. EP2's brand line is protected.
