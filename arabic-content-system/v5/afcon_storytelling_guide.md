# AFCON and sports storytelling guide

For true football stories told in Egyptian Arabic over archive footage or to camera
(the «حكاية إصابة · جيل الذهب» series and anything like it). An exercise explainer moves
from a problem to a mechanism to an action. A sports story moves from a **question** through
**stakes** and a **turn** to a **consequence**. Don't write one with the other's logic.

## 1. Before writing: the story sheet (fill every line or don't write)

| Question | Aboutrika 2010 (EP01) | Zidan 2008 (EP02) | Gedo 2010 (GEDO) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** is the story about? | Egypt's biggest star | a player who skipped a title once | an unknown local-league striker |
| **What does he want / what's in the way?** | to play / a stress fracture with an uncertain timeline | to finally be champion / an ankle injury before the knockouts | none: the story is a paradox, not a conflict |
| **Stakes** (why it mattered then) | Egypt just lost the World Cup play-off in Omdurman; they're defending two titles | he missed 2006 by choice; this was his chance | Egypt without Aboutrika |
| **The turn** | *he* asked not to be selected (revealed 7 years later) | in the final, against the same Cameroon he scored twice against in the opener | his goals grow heavier as the tournament goes on |
| **Consequence** | Egypt win 6 of 6, including 4–0 v Algeria | he wins the ball off Cameroon's captain → the winning goal | top scorer without a single start |
| **Insight (why tell it today)** | readiness is a decision, not a feeling | the duel is the last thing an ankle gets back | the substitute's advantage is fresh legs. Use it or lose it |
| **Verified facts / sources** | see NOTES in `scripts/v5_final/EP01.md` | `EP02.md` | `GEDO.md` |

If there's no real conflict (Gedo), don't invent one. Find the true paradox and let the facts escalate.

## 2. Structure: choose by the story, not by habit

| Shape | Use when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Delayed reveal**: open on the outcome, hold back the reason | the *why* is the surprising part | EP01: «…وأبو تريكة ما لعبش ولا دقيقة. والسبب اتعرف بعدها بسبع سنين.» |
| **Missed chance → second chance** | the character had something to make up for | EP02: 2006 skipped → 2008 |
| **Paradox → escalation** | the fact itself is strange, and it gets bigger | GEDO: never started → goals in the quarter-final, semi-final and final |
| **Cold open on the decisive moment** | the moment is famous and the backstory isn't | (v4 EP02, now the alternative) |
| **Bookend / rematch** | the same opponent or place returns | EP02: Cameroon in the opener and in the final |

Within one batch, no two stories should use the same shape.

## 3. Rules that keep it human

1. **Stakes before events.** One line of *why it mattered then* is worth five dates. EP01 v3 gave «21 نوفمبر 2009… 10 يناير» before the viewer knew why to care. v5 gives «مصر لسه خارجة من أم درمان، وكاس العالم راح للجزاير» first.
2. **Dates only when they carry tension.** "40 days, when the tournament ends on day 39" carries tension. "21 November" by itself doesn't. Put the rest on screen cards.
3. **Hold the reveal to the middle, and pay it.** If the hook says "the reason came out later", the middle has to give the reason, plainly.
4. **Withholding ≠ hiding basics.** Say who and when early. Hold back only the turn.
5. **Concrete verbs for the decisive moment, present tense.** «زيدان يدخل عليه، ياخدها، ويحطها لأبو تريكة.» Shift to present tense only for the key action.
6. **Let the score do the emotion.** «أربعة صفر على الجزاير» needs no adjective. Never call a match «تاريخي» or «ملحمة». Say what happened that makes it so.
7. **One honest uncertainty, said once.** «فيه تلات روايات، ومفيش واحدة مؤكدة.» Then move on.
8. **The science bridge is one or two lines, and it comes out of the story.** The bone line in EP01; the duel line in EP02. It's never a lecture.
9. **End on an insight specific to this story.** Not "and that's why he's a legend". EP01: «أبو تريكة اتعرف بالجوان الكبيرة. بس في 2010، أهم حاجة عملها كانت كلمة واحدة: لسه.»
10. **Series links are true and optional.** EP01 → GEDO (same tournament). Never claim Gedo "replaced" Aboutrika. They played different roles, and that wasn't documented as a replacement.

## 4. Honesty rules (non-negotiable)

- No invented dialogue, thoughts, feelings, dressing-room scenes or motives. Paraphrase only what was reported, and attribute it («حكى بعدها»، «قال إنه جاي…»).
- When sources disagree (the minute of the 2008 winner: 76'/77'; Gedo's final goal: 84'/85'), use a range («قبل النهاية بربع ساعة تقريبًا»).
- Rivalries: give the result, not the gloating. The 2010 semi-final ended with three Algerian red cards and a referee who was later suspended. The script states the score without the word «انتقام».
- Keep a «ممنوع يتقال» list in every story file, and check the final draft against it.

## 5. Failure patterns (rewrite on sight)

| Pattern | v3 example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Timeline narration | «21 نوفمبر 2009… وفي آخر ديسمبر… والبطولة بتبدأ 10 يناير.» | stakes first, then one date that carries tension |
| Over-explaining uncertainty | listing all three injury accounts aloud (~8 s) | one line, details on a card |
| An ambiguous hook | «فاته لقب 2010 كله» (Egypt won it) | «مصر كسبت… وأبو تريكة ما لعبش ولا دقيقة» |
| Arguing with no one | «ده مش تفسير لجوانه. ده وصف للدور.» | «الأرقام دي عامة، بس جدو استغل الميزة دي لآخرها.» |
| An unsupported attribution | «والكاف بعدها سمّته» | drop it, or attribute only what the source says |
| Empty documentary language | «دخل التاريخ»، «لحظة لا تُنسى»، «محدش كان يتوقع» | blocked by the lint (`sports_cliches`); say the fact |
| Fragment drumbeat | «جدو. ست ماتشات. وخمس جوان.» throughout | at most one run of short sentences, at the turn |

## 6. Length

These stories run 50–60 s (estimated). That's longer than v3's stated 45–55 s, because a real
arc needs the stakes line. If you want 45–55 s, cut the science bridge first, then the
uncertainty line. Never cut the stakes.
