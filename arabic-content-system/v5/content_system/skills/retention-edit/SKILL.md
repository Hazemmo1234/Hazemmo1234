# Retention edit and compression

Read `content_system/guides/retention.md`.
1. **Word budget:** max words ≈ target seconds × 1.95. Lint shows the estimate.
2. **Dead zones:** about 8 s with nothing new means a cut, or a demo/card instead of narration.
3. **Middle hook:** is it a real turn, reveal or escalation from the content? If it's a dramatic line with nothing behind it, cut it.
4. **Cut order:** repeated ideas → spoken disclaimers (move them on screen) → dates that carry no tension → adjectives → the second example. **Never cut:** the stakes line, the turn, the qualifier that keeps a claim honest.
5. **Premature endings:** a line that sounds final before the end is where people swipe.
6. Log every cut and its reason in the worknote.
