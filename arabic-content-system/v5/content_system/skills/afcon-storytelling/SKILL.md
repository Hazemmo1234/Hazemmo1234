# AFCON and sports storytelling

Read `afcon_storytelling_guide.md` first, then `content_system/guides/fact_check_protocol.md` §3.

1. **Fill the story sheet** (guide §1). Every line needs a verified fact behind it. If there's no conflict, find the true paradox.
2. **Research the context that creates the stakes:** what happened just before (Omdurman before 2010), what the character missed before (2006 for Zidan), recurring opponents (Cameroon in 2008). This is usually where the drama really is.
3. **Choose a shape** (guide §2). It must differ from the other stories in the batch.
4. **Write the stakes line before any date.** Keep only the dates that carry tension; the rest go on cards.
5. **Hold the turn to the middle**, then state it plainly and attribute it.
6. **Narrate the decisive action in present tense with concrete verbs.**
7. **A science bridge of 1–2 lines** that comes out of the story (the bone, the ankle, the fresh legs).
8. **End on the insight specific to this story.** Optionally tease the next story, if the link is true.
9. **Check the draft against its «ممنوع يتقال» list** and the guide's failure patterns. The lint blocks the sports clichés.
