# Narrative structures

Pick the structure the topic needs. Don't put every script through the same formula.
Second-pass check: within one batch, no more than half the scripts should share a structure.

| Structure | Beats | Best for | Round 1 usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Moment → mechanism → implication** | the exact moment · what happens inside · what to do | biomechanics | A1 |
| **Myth vs reality** | belief · the fact that breaks it · the practical shift | misconceptions | «مالوش لازمة» series |
| **Recognition → model → test** | "this happened to you" · simple model · how to check | rehab misconceptions | B1 |
| **Number → mechanism → do-it-now** | surprising figure · why · something to try today | prevention | A2 |
| **Comparison with stakes** | option A vs option B · outcomes · the rule that falls out | treatment choices | B2 |
| **Demonstration and interpretation** | do the test · what the result means · what it doesn't mean | self-tests | B3 |
| **Story: decision under uncertainty** | context · injury · the decision · the science · the outcome | athlete stories | EP01 |
| **Story: payoff-first** | the famous moment · rewind · how it was possible | athlete stories | EP02 |
| **Story: paradox** | paradoxical fact · how it happened · what the role teaches | non-injury stories | GEDO |
| **Mistake → consequence → correction** | the common mistake · its cost · the fix | gym mistakes | (future) |
| **Progressive reveal / list** | item, item, bigger item | short series | (future) |

## Timing skeletons (estimates)

- **≤25 s (series):** hook 0–2 s · 2–3 beats of 4–6 s · signature close 3–4 s.
- **35–45 s (educational):** hook 0–3 s · setup ≤8 s · middle hook around 15–20 s · payoff
  and action in the last 8–10 s.
- **45–55 s (story):** cold open 0–4 s · context ≤10 s · turn around 20–25 s · science
  bridge ≤10 s · payoff in the last 8 s.
