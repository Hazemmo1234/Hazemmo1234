# Editorial rubric v5 (10 dimensions × 10 = 100)

The same rubric scores the original and the rewrite. **These are editorial diagnostics, not
predictions of virality, reach or retention.** Give each score a one-line justification that
quotes the line it rests on. Mark judgement as (J) and checkable facts as (F).

| # | Dimension | 1–4 | 5–7 | 8–10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening strength and specificity | generic, ambiguous or misleading | clear but predictable | specific to this topic, a reason to care within 2 s, an honest gap |
| 2 | Narrative coherence | a jumble, or the order fights understanding | follows, with bumps | every line follows from the last; chronology is clear by ear |
| 3 | Story/explanation progression | a list of facts | builds partly | each beat adds something; stakes → turn → consequence (story) or problem → mechanism → action (educational) |
| 4 | Middle-hook quality | none, or artificial | present but weak | a real turn or reveal that comes out of the content and is paid off |
| 5 | Natural Egyptian Arabic | MSA or translated | mostly natural, some tics | sounds like a person talking; no tics over their caps |
| 6 | Originality and distinctiveness | a generic take | familiar topic with a sharper angle | an angle or connection the viewer hasn't heard (judgement) |
| 7 | Concision and pacing | over length, filler | within ±10%, removable lines | on target; every line earns its place |
| 8 | Payoff and audience value | no takeaway, or a default referral | an abstract takeaway | the ending pays off the opening; specific insight or action |
| 9 | Factual/scientific integrity | wrong or overstated | minor overstatement or a missing qualifier | every claim matches its source at the stated strength |
| 10 | Spoken delivery | hard to say, breath problems | sayable with effort | flows aloud; understandable on one hearing |

**Acceptance bar (v5):** total ≥75, integrity ≥8, no dimension below 6, lint 0 FAIL,
regression cases passing. A script under the bar is revised again or listed with its
limiting factor.
