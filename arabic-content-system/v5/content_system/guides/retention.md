# Middle hooks and retention

A short-form script loses people at transitions: when the hook's question has been
answered, or when explanation runs too long without anything new. A middle hook is the
moment the script hands the viewer a **new** reason to stay. It has to grow out of the
content itself. A dramatic line dropped in to satisfy a template doesn't count.

Working heuristic (not a measured law): something new should arrive about every 5–8
seconds: a fact, a visual, a turn, a demonstration. The growthquest copywriting skill
states the same heuristic. Neither source has retention data behind it.

## Middle-hook moves

| Move | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Escalating number** | The first fact surprises; the second is bigger or more concrete | A1: two moments → «عدّى أقصى قوة… بمرة ونص تقريبًا» |
| **Answer opens a second question** | Resolve the hook, then raise the question the answer creates | B1: «فاحنا عرفنا ليه… طب تعرف إزاي إنك وصلت؟» |
| **Demonstration** | The viewer does something and sees it on their own body | A2/B3: the wall test |
| **Turn of the story** | A decision, a reversal, or someone saying something unexpected | EP01: «شحاتة كان مستعد يستناه. بس…» |
| **Callback / echo** | Bring back a word from the hook with new weight | EP02: «لسه مش عارف» echoing EP01's «لسه» |
| **Stakes reveal** | What it costs if you ignore it | B2: «بعد 6 شهور التحسن رجع لورا» |
| **Prediction then data** | Let the viewer guess, then show the result | B2: «ناس وقفت… وناس كمّلت. اتحسنوا قد بعض.» |

## Checks (stage 9, retention edit)

- **Where is the hook answered?** If it's answered before about 40% of the script, a second
  question must already be open. If the hook is answered in the last line only, check that
  the middle isn't just delay.
- **Dead zones:** any stretch of more than ~8 seconds of explanation with no new fact,
  visual or turn. Cut it or add a demo.
- **Disclaimer interruptions:** does a caveat break the flow? Move it on screen, or fold it
  into one clause.
- **Premature ending:** does a sentence sound like the end before the end? («وده اللي
  حصل.») Viewers swipe there.
- **Unresolved promises:** list every implicit question the script raises and where it's
  answered.
- **Repetition:** does any line restate an earlier one? Cut the weaker one.

## Anti-patterns

- «بس استنى، لسه فيه أهم حاجة» with nothing big behind it.
- Holding back essential info to fake tension (for example, naming the test only at the end).
- A middle hook with the same sentence shape as the hook.
