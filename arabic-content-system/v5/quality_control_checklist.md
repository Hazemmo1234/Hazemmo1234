# Quality control checklist

Apply it **to the final file**, line by line, not from memory. Tick only what you checked.

## Every script
- [ ] **Reason to exist:** one sentence says what the viewer gains. It's written in the worknote.
- [ ] **Opening:** specific to this topic; it couldn't open a different video; it gives a reason to care within 2 s; it promises only what the script delivers.
- [ ] **Progression:** cover each beat in turn. Each one adds something new (fact, turn, demo, consequence).
- [ ] **Transitions:** none of «وده اللي حصل»، «بس اللي حصل بعد كده…», no "but then everything changed".
- [ ] **Middle hook:** a real turn, reveal or escalation from the content, and it's paid off later.
- [ ] **Egyptian by ear:** read aloud once. No line needs a second breath. No MSA connectors.
- [ ] **Tics within caps:** «مش X… Y» ≤1 · «X: Y» ≤2 · «…» ≤2 · no runs of 3+ fragments · questions ≤2–3 · no clichés (lint).
- [ ] **Payoff:** the ending answers the opening, or gives one action. A referral only with a named red flag.
- [ ] **Concise:** inside the target estimate (lint); every line survives the "if I cut it, what's lost?" test.
- [ ] **Facts:** every claim is in the claim ledger with a label; the final wording re-checked (compression changes claims).
- [ ] **On screen:** caveats, n, sources and conflicting minutes are on cards or in NOTES, not in the mouth.

## AFCON / sports stories, additionally
- [ ] Story sheet complete (who · want/obstacle · stakes · turn · consequence · insight).
- [ ] The stakes line comes before the first date; only dates that carry tension are spoken.
- [ ] The turn is held for the middle, stated plainly and attributed.
- [ ] Chronology is clear on one hearing (who before when, cause before effect).
- [ ] There's a real arc, not a sequence of facts (stakes → setback/turn → consequence).
- [ ] No invented dialogue, feelings, motives or scenes; reported speech is paraphrased and attributed.
- [ ] Discrepant facts use ranges; rivalries state results without gloating.
- [ ] The insight is specific to this story, not "legend / history / unforgettable".
- [ ] The «ممنوع يتقال» list was checked against the final text.

## The whole batch
- [ ] No two openings start the same way; at most 2 approved reels open with a question.
- [ ] No two stories share a structure.
- [ ] No two endings in a series share a shape.
- [ ] Series conditions are unique across episodes.
- [ ] Measured change from the source is reported (word-level similarity), so "nothing changed" can't pass silently.

## Before filming
- [ ] Read 3× with a stopwatch; use the median. If it's over target, run `/retention-edit`.
