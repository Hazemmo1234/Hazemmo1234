# Hook engineering

The hook is the first 1–3 seconds (about 3–16 words). It does one job: make *this* viewer
feel that the next 30 seconds are about them and will give them something they don't have.

## The hook–payoff contract

Write the payoff first. A hook is acceptable only if the script delivers exactly what it
implies, and no more. If the hook promises "the reason", the script must give *the reason*,
not "it depends, see a professional".

## Hook types (generate across types, not variations of one sentence)

| Type | Shape | Use when | Example (this project) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Defensible contradiction** | Common belief → the fact that breaks it | There's a real myth and solid evidence | «الكارديو مالوش لازمة!» → resolved in the next line |
| **Specific moment** | Name the exact moment the problem happens | Mechanism-based topics | «فيه لحظتين وانت بتجري سرعة، الهامسترينج فيهم شايل أتقل حمل في الحركة كلها.» |
| **Recognition scenario** | Describe the viewer's situation precisely | Recurring problems the viewer has lived | «الوجع راح، رجعت تلعب، واتصبت تاني؟» |
| **Hard number** | One verified figure that surprises | You have a clean, defensible statistic | «من كل 3 رياضيين لووا كاحلهم، واحد بيلويه تاني في خلال سنة.» |
| **Demonstration / do-it-now** | Instruct an action the viewer can do while watching | Self-tests, experiments | «اقف قدام حيطة، وفي 30 ثانية هتعرف…» |
| **Comparison with stakes** | Two options the viewer weighs, with a real outcome | Treatment / training choices | «حقنة ولا تمرين تقيل؟ بعد 6 شهور، الفرق كان واضح.» |
| **Story cold-open** | Start inside the moment | True stories with a payoff | «الالتحام اللي جاب جول نهائي 2008، عمله لاعب كاحله متصاب من 6 أيام.» |
| **Consequence-first** | Show the cost, then the cause | Mistakes with real costs | «كعبك بيترفع في السكوات؟ ابدأ من هنا.» |

## Scoring each candidate (1–5 each, write the numbers down)

1. **Clarity:** understood on first hearing, with no context and no sound-off confusion?
2. **Specificity:** could this hook open a video on a different topic? If yes, score ≤2.
3. **Relevance:** does the target viewer recognise themselves within 2 seconds?
4. **Curiosity:** is there a real gap the script closes? (empty mystery doesn't count)
5. **Credibility:** would a sceptical physio nod, or wince?
6. **Originality:** is this angle different from the top 10 Egyptian fitness reels on the topic? If you can't check, score from judgement and mark it as judgement.
7. **Speakability:** can Hazem say it in one breath without sounding like he's reading?
8. **Payoff alignment:** does the ending deliver exactly this?

Pick the highest total, but **any 1 on Credibility or Payoff alignment disqualifies the
candidate**. Record the top 3 and why the winner won (see `scripts/v4_worknotes/`).

## Rejection rules

- Empty clickbait («محدش هيقولك ده»).
- A hook that only works if the script later walks it back. The series refrain «X مالوش
  لازمة!» is allowed because the next line qualifies it within 2 seconds. Without that, it
  would be misleading.
- Twenty rewordings of one sentence counted as twenty hooks.
- A question whose honest answer is "no" or "it depends", used to fake tension.

## Pairing the visual

Every hook gets a visual instruction: what the viewer sees in frame 1 (freeze frame, a line
being drawn, Hazem at the wall, the score card). The visual hook and the verbal hook should
point at the same thing.
