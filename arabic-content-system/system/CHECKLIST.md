# Script quality checklist (use on every script)

Print it, or paste it under the draft. Tick only what you have actually checked.

## Before writing
- [ ] One target viewer, described as a situation («لاعب كورة الشد الخلفي بيرجعله»), not a demographic
- [ ] Core message in ONE sentence
- [ ] Why this deserves 30 seconds: what will the viewer understand that they didn't?
- [ ] Every factual claim listed and labelled (VERIFIED / MECHANISM / PRINCIPLE / CARRIED)

## Hook
- [ ] At least 4 candidates from at least 3 different hook types, scored
- [ ] The winner could NOT open a video on another topic
- [ ] The payoff delivers exactly what the hook implies
- [ ] Sayable in one breath (≤ 16 words in the first sentence)
- [ ] A visual for frame 1 is specified

## Middle
- [ ] Something new arrives about every 5–8 s (fact, visual, turn, demo)
- [ ] There's a middle hook that comes out of the content
- [ ] No stretch longer than ~8 s with nothing new
- [ ] Caveats are on screen, or folded into one short spoken clause
- [ ] No line repeats an earlier one

## Language (read it aloud)
- [ ] No MSA connectors (الذي، لذلك، حيث، هذا، سوف، يجب، فإن)
- [ ] At most 1 «مش X… Y» contrast, used for the central myth only
- [ ] No stock phrases (see BANNED_PATTERNS)
- [ ] Rhythm varies (not all fragments, not all long)
- [ ] English terms written the way gym people say them

## Science
- [ ] Each number matches its source (absolute numbers preferred; n on screen if small)
- [ ] No absolute claims beyond the evidence
- [ ] No fear framing about pain, posture or imaging
- [ ] «مش بنقول» notes written for anything a viewer might wrongly infer

## Ending
- [ ] Resolves the hook or gives one action for today
- [ ] Not a default "see a professional". If a referral is needed, it names the red flag
- [ ] Not the same shape as another ending in this batch
- [ ] CTA (if any) is one, specific, and matches what the reel delivered

## Final
- [ ] Estimated duration inside target (`tools/lint_scripts.py`), then 3 timed read-alouds, take the median
- [ ] Lint: 0 FAIL
- [ ] Rubric ≥ 80, accuracy ≥ 8, no Low band
