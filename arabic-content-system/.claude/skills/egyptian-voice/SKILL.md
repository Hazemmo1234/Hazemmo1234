# Egyptian spoken voice (stages 6 and 8)

Read `system/STYLE_GUIDE_EGYPTIAN.md` and `system/BANNED_PATTERNS.md`.

## Drafting (stage 6)
- Follow the beat list. One idea per sentence. Concrete moment before the concept.
- Write numbers the way they're spoken. Precise figures go to `PRODUCTION` as on-screen text.
- Use the script file format (`system/SCRIPT_FORMAT.md`).

## Spoken edit (stage 8): read every line aloud, then:
1. Replace MSA connectors and written-register words.
2. Count «مش» contrasts. Keep at most one, for the central myth. Rewrite the rest as direct
   statements.
3. Find lines that "argue with no one" and state the positive fact instead.
4. Break any sentence you can't say in one breath.
5. Vary rhythm. Merge fragment chains into one full sentence.
6. Check that every English term is one gym people actually use.
7. **Protect good lines.** Lines in the voice fingerprint (style guide) or listed as
   «سطور عاجباك» in the brief are only changed if they're inaccurate. Log every change to a
   protected line, with the reason.

Don't add slang, jokes or drama to "sound Egyptian". Natural means clear and conversational.
