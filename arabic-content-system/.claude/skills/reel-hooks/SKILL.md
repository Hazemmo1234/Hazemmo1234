# Hook engineering (stage 3)

Read `system/HOOK_ENGINEERING.md` first.

1. **Write the payoff first** (what the last 8 seconds deliver).
2. **Generate ≥4 candidates from ≥3 different types** (contradiction, specific moment,
   recognition scenario, hard number, demonstration, comparison with stakes, story
   cold-open, consequence-first). Rewordings of the same idea count as one candidate.
3. **Score each 1–5** on: clarity, specificity, relevance, curiosity, credibility,
   originality (mark it as judgement), speakability, payoff alignment. Show the table.
4. **Disqualify** anything scoring 1 on credibility or payoff alignment, and anything that
   would fit a video on a different topic.
5. **Pick a winner and write two lines of rationale.** Keep the runner-up as `[ALT]` for A/B
   testing if the format allows.
6. **Pair the visual:** what frame 1 shows.
7. **Check against the batch:** don't open the same way as another script in this round
   (`tools/lint_scripts.py` flags repeated openers).

If the original hook is already strong, say so and keep it, tightening at most a few words.
